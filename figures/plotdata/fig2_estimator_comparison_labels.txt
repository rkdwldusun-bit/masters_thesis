caption: Note: Same treated crops, controls and period as Figure 1. All rows cover the same post-availability horizon, e = 0 to 9: group-time and imputation rows average the event-time estimates; the two TWFE rows are static coefficients estimated with treated observations beyond e = 9 excluded, and are benchmarks only. Intervals: 95% crop-level wild bootstrap (group-time and imputation; custom procedure); TWFE: estimate ± 1.96 crop-clustered standard errors (normal approximation; restricted wild cluster bootstrap p-values are in the results table). Estimates are associations, not established causal effects. Source: KOSIS Crop Production Survey (national series); author's calculations.
