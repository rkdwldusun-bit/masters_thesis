caption: Note: Custom group-time difference-in-differences with clean not-yet-treated and never-treated controls; reference year = each treated crop's final clean pre-pilot year (e = −8 to −4). Treated: Soybean, Onion, Sweet potato, Corn, Garlic, Spring potato, Red pepper (nationwide availability 2012–2015). Controls: 24 listed annual crop series, each used only before its own first pilot; 22 contribute, and 8–11 remain at e = 9. Crop-specific pilot-to-national transition years are omitted (not estimated); the e = −5 estimate rests on two crops. Estimates are associations, not established causal effects. Vertical bars: 95% crop-level wild bootstrap intervals (Webb weights, 9,999 draws; custom procedure). Source: KOSIS Crop Production Survey (national series); author's calculations.
